CHG-002 — A payment above the newest settlement amount is rejected

Status: Approved Date: 2026-10-20 Applies to: services/payment-service only. The Struts monolith under src/main is not changed and keeps accepting the payment. Rules affected: PAY-R15 (v1 superseded by v2); PAY-R07, PAY-R08, PAY-R13 and PAY-R14 (unchanged, but no longer reached on this path)

Why

The legacy issue action has no business checks. It reads the newest settlement of the claim only to take its id and, when amount is missing or not a number, its amount (src/main/java/com/northstar/claims/web/PaymentIssueAction.java:23, src/main/java/com/northstar/claims/web/PaymentIssueAction.java:30-31). Any amount the operator types is written, however far above the settlement it is: 999999 for claim 1, settled at 0.00, is accepted twice (live:issue-no-business-checks#2, live:issue-no-business-checks#3). A cheque for more than the agreed settlement can leave the desk with no warning.

Before (legacy, and the service up to this change)

Precondition parity/preconditions/payment.sql#chg-002-above-settlement: fresh seed plus settlement 121 for claim 119 with a settlement amount of 1000.

POST /claims/payment/issue.do as supervisor with claimId=119, payeeName=Over Cap Claimant, amount=1000.01, paymentMethod=CHECK:

  • legacy: status 200, issue.jsp, fields paymentAmount 1000.01, checkNumber CHK-61, paymentStatus ISSUED; PAYMENT row 61 written with amount 1000.01 and settlement 121. Observed live against the legacy app on 8080 by make payment-spec-proof, case chg-002-above-settlement (docs/specs/SPEC-PAY-001-proof.json). No transcript covers this request: the scenario is declared with its own request block in parity/routes.json, payment_scenarios.chg-002-above-settlement.
  • service before this change: the same answer and the same row (PAY-R15 (v1)).

After (service only)

POST or GET /payment/issue checks the amount after the claim fallback and the newest-settlement lookup, and before the payment id is read:

  • the payment amount is the parsed amount, or the settlement amount when amount is missing or not parseable — unchanged (PAY-R07, PAY-R08);
  • when it is greater than the newest settlement's settlement_amount, the answer is status 200, screen issue, fields, display and links empty, and errors exactly ["payment.amount.aboveSettlement"]. No PAYMENT row is written and no payment id is used;
  • an amount equal to the settlement amount is accepted; NaN is never greater, so it is accepted; a negative amount is accepted;
  • a claim with no settlement still ends on the error screen (PAY-R06), because the lookup comes first.

The same request as above now answers the validation key and writes nothing.

Unchanged by this record: the claim fallback to 119, the amount fallback, the newest-settlement choice, the next id (max + 1 over the whole table), the check number CHK-<id>, the issued date 2019-04-03, the status ISSUED, the GET write, the operator check and every read screen.
