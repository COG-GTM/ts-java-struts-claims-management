# Parity

Generated by `make parity` (tools/parity/settlement_parity.py and
tools/parity/payment_parity.py through tools/parity/report.py); the same
verdicts, with each scenario's request, precondition, expected and actual
response and the rules it is evidence for, are in parity/report.json and
the interactive parity/report.html.

Each `settlement_*` row replays a `transcripts/settlement_*.json` scenario
against the settlement service on 8083; each `payment_*` row replays a
payment scenario of `parity/routes.json` (a `transcripts/payment_*.json`
transcript, or a request recorded in `docs/specs/SPEC-PAY-001-live.json`)
against the payment service on 8084 after applying the scenario's declared
precondition from `parity/preconditions/payment`. Both compare the four
things ADR-001:82-90 and ADR-002 ("How parity is judged") call parity:
status class, business fields, validation keys, db state.

Scenarios listed under `approved_differences` in `parity/routes.json` are
judged against their `docs/changes/CHG-nnn` record and reported as CHANGED,
and still fail on anything the record does not approve.
